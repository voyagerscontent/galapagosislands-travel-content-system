# ENTITY RULES — brand-neutral (resolved from SITE_CONFIG BRAND BLOCK)

Hard naming/relationship rules. Apply at Drafting, enforce at Truth Check + Auditor.
All brand-specific values come from `config/SITE_CONFIG.md`; this file uses tokens so
it can be reused for any brand by editing SITE_CONFIG only.

## Brand & domain
- The site/publisher is **`{{BRAND}}`** (spoken/alt form **`{{BRAND_ALT}}`**). Use it consistently. **Never** "Galapagos Travel Center".
- Domain is **`{{DOMAIN}}`**. **Never** write `galapagosislands.com` (links, emails, citations).
- If `PUBLISHER` differs from `{{BRAND}}` in SITE_CONFIG, disclose the relationship transparently and keep editorial independence explicit ("useful whether or not you book with us"). If `PUBLISHER` is blank or equals the brand, write brand-first with no parent-agency claims.

## Editorial independence & operators
- This is an **independent editorial guide**, NOT a single-operator marketing vehicle.
- **Never favor one operator.** Vessels/operators are named factually and comparatively; none owns the platform or gets the last word.
- Do not present any single DMC/operator as the site's owner or sole operator. Third-party operators may be referenced factually.
- **Luxury is a property of the vessel's full experience** (service, guiding, cuisine, crew, comfort, perks) — **never** of which islands it visits (see HARD TRUTHS, GALAPAGOS_FACTS_ADDENDUM §0 HT-2).

## Contributors
- Attribute expert voices only from `{{CONTRIBUTORS}}` (SITE_CONFIG). If empty, write in brand editorial voice with no named contributor. Never invent a contributor or title.

## Conversion
- The page closes with the **subtle dual CTA** (see `guardrails/CTA_BLOCK.md`): travelers → **Voyagers Travel Company** (full planning), trade → **Latin Trails** (Galápagos DMC). This footer naming of the two operators behind the guide is the ONE sanctioned exception to no-operator-favoritism. Never instant "Book Now"; elsewhere on the page operators stay factual and comparative.

## Sources vs. voice (brand-neutral sourcing)
- External authorities (e.g. Charles Darwin Foundation, Galápagos Conservancy, Galápagos Conservation Trust, DPNG) may be **cited as sources** for facts. **Never** write as if the content is published by them or in their voice; the only publishing brand is `{{BRAND}}`.

# CONTENT FIDELITY — {{DOMAIN}}

The second Editorial Guardian (Content): the draft must say only what the brief and facts support, structured for the declared page type — no scope drift, no padding.

## Rules
1. **Stay on the brief.** Cover the brief's topic, persona, funnel stage, and CTA — nothing the brief did not authorize.
2. **No padding (Guardian of Content).** Every paragraph earns its place; cut filler. Shorter beats longer.
3. **Structure = the page type.** Follow the section order from `TEMPLATE-SPEC.md` for the chosen page type; don't invent sections that break the schema/AIO plan.
4. **Answer-first.** Lead each section with the direct answer, then support. Question-form headings where the data maps to a real query.
5. **Match content to funnel stage.** Awareness = useful/evocative; consideration = comparative/honest; decision = confident/frictionless. No hard "Book Now" on awareness content.
6. **Schema mirrors visible content** exactly (FAQ answers, itinerary/offer data, review/rating).
7. **One primary CTA**, reused verbatim; one secondary lead magnet; a human fallback. No CTA overload.

# Galápagos Travel FAQs

**How much does a Galápagos cruise cost?**
Most cruises run $2,500 to $20,000+ per person for 4 to 15 days, depending on tier [source: MASTER_FACTS; galapagos_ships.xlsx]. Two costs are always extra: the $200 national park fee and the ~$20 INGALA transit card. Mainland flights add about $400–$500. We'll show you exactly what each fare includes.

**Is it safe?**
Yes, within a tightly regulated system. Every vessel runs GNPS-certified guides, fixed landing schedules, and biosecurity rules; landings are by panga with trained crew [source: MASTER_FACTS]. Several ships also carry a doctor on board — tell us if that matters and we'll point you to them.

**What if my plans change — can I cancel?**
Deposit and cancellation terms vary by operator; some offer flexible or reduced deposits. We'll lay out the actual terms for your shortlisted vessels before you commit, and we always recommend trip-cancellation insurance to cover what the operator can't.

**Could a flight delay make me miss the ship?**
It's the most common avoidable problem. Always fly to the mainland a day before departure [source: MASTER_FACTS]. Mainland-flight cancellations are outside an operator's control, and most can't refund a missed departure — travel insurance covers that gap.

**Do I need travel insurance?**
We strongly recommend comprehensive travel and medical insurance for every Galápagos trip, including trip-cancellation cover. It's the single best protection against weather, flight, and health disruptions on a remote, multi-day voyage.
